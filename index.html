How I work

Troubleshoot across layers

I work from symptom to cause across application, container, host, network, driver and hardware layers instead of treating an error message as the diagnosis.

Build with constraints

Many of my projects use repurposed or limited hardware, which has made resource planning, compatibility and practical trade-offs part of the engineering process.

Document what is real

I separate completed work from ongoing experiments and avoid presenting research or planned functionality as already implemented.

Security by design

My projects increasingly include least privilege, development/production separation, controlled execution and deliberate handling of credentials and private data.
